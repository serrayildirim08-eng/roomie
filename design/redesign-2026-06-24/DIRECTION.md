# Roomie — visual direction (2026-06-24)

**The thesis:** "a sunlit kitchen corkboard, not a fintech dashboard." The old
DNA (linen, espresso, terracotta, Fraunces) was *right* — it just landed flat.
This isn't a new palette; it's the same palette finally given hierarchy and air.

## Why it was "ugly af" (the diagnosis)
- **No contrast.** Oat cards (#F4EDE1) on linen (#FBF7F0) were nearly the same
  colour — every screen read as one muddy beige sheet.
- **No hierarchy.** Balance, expenses, chores all sat at the same weight. Nothing
  led the eye.
- **The fintech tell.** Glowy terracotta drop-shadows on buttons.
- **A lonely 34px bold title** top-left = generic app, not editorial.

## What changed
1. **Cards now lift.** Surfaces went to near-white paper (#FFFCF6) so they float
   off the linen. Soft, warm, low shadows — never the Material glow.
2. **Fraunces does the singing.** The serif now carries greeting, screen titles,
   the brain-box placeholder, chore names, money amounts — the "home" moments.
   Nunito stays for labels and rows. One clear voice per weight.
3. **Hierarchy by structure, not colour.** Kicker → serif title → hairline rule
   on every screen. Money leads with ONE big balance card; everything else is a
   quiet list. One focus per screen, as locked.
4. **Corkboard, literally.** The brain-box on Home is a pinned note (terracotta
   tack). Avatars are warm initials, not stock icons.
5. **Emoji gone.** Replaced 👋🤍🌿 with Fraunces dinkus/§/⁂ ornament and soft
   italic asides. Line icons (1.4px) everywhere — no filled glyphs.
6. **No score, ever.** Chores show "your turn" / "you did it" / "moved on from"
   and a chronological history. No counts, points, streaks, or shame.

**Net:** warmer, calmer, fewer words, more white space — editorial, not SaaS.
