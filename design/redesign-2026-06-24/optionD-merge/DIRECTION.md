# Direction D — Editorial Header × Joyful Cards (the merge)

**One line:** A strong forest-green editorial header sets context AND carries the screen's hero number; below it flows a body of big, tactile, identity-colored cards that are organized as tappable modules, not competing heroes. One confident hybrid — structure up top, life in the body.

## Taken FROM B (structure)
- The bold designed header zone on every screen: app bar (hamburger + household switcher + profile avatar), big editorial title, member faces, and the screen's most important info.
- Information discipline: segmented controls, filter chips, day-grouped sections, and a chronological history timeline.
- "Density via alignment & rhythm, not shrinking" — type stays big, organization carries the load.

## Taken FROM C (life)
- Big rounded cards (24–28px radius) with generous padding that fill the body.
- Oversized friendly numbers (Baloo 2, 56px hero balance), member identity colors, the warm "your turn" spotlight, and money-confetti texture in the header.
- Chunky 5px drop-edge buttons for that tactile, pressable, Duolingo/Cash App feel.

## Conflicts RESOLVED (deliberate choices)
1. **Two heroes → one.** B's header-hero and C's hero-card both wanted the big number. Resolved: the **header owns the hero** (e.g. +€15 lives in the green block). Body cards demote to organized, tappable modules so nothing competes.
2. **Identity colors.** C used coral for Kai (clashed with the coral spotlight/FAB). Adopted B's set: You=forest, Kai=blue, Vfya=pink — legible, no collisions.
3. **Chunky buttons everywhere → primary only.** C's drop-edge is delightful but noisy if global. Kept it for primary actions (mark done / settle / add to list / FAB) only; everything else stays calm.
4. **Texture dialed down.** C's confetti kept, but moved into the header at 16% opacity so the body stays clean.

## Two bright-pop buttons (Serra, 2026-06-25)
- **ADD (FAB) = yellow/gold #FFCB2E** on every screen — the add affordance is now unmistakably yellow, not green/coral.
- **SETTLE / pay (Money) = hot pink #FF2E88** — the standalone "get paid back" moment. Chosen over Home's CTA because it's a real, distinct primary action and hot pink doesn't collide with the coral "your turn · mark done" chore button.
- Everything else stays deep-green lead; these two are the only bright pops, and they read as two intentional, visually distinct buttons.

## Locked rules honored
No streaks, points, scores, leaderboards, or shame. Chores use soft language ("your turn · no rush", "moved on from it", "you did it") + chronological history timeline only.
